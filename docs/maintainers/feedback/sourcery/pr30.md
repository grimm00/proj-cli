## Overall Comments

- The late-binding pattern around `get_client`/`get_package_imports` and the APIClient/Config re-exports is fairly subtle; consider adding a short design comment in `helpers.py` or `projects/__init__.py` describing the test-patching strategy so future maintainers understand why it’s structured this way.
- Now that inventory export maps `local_path` → `path` for the API, consider a follow-up cleanup to standardize on a single field name internally (either `path` or `local_path`) to reduce the chance of similar mismatches elsewhere.
- Several uses of `assert_command_exists` omit `expected_text`, so they only assert a zero exit code; you might want to consistently provide an expected snippet for each command to ensure the help output is actually for the intended subcommand.
